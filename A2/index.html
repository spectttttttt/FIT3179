Global Restaurant Trends Dashboard

Exploring the relationship between cuisine popularity, ratings, and global distribution

Distribution of Restaurants Across the World

The map illustrates restaurant concentrations around the world, revealing where culinary cultures thrive most. Densely populated urban regions like East Asia, Europe, and North America show strong restaurant presence, reflecting cultural diversity and high consumer demand. Areas with sparse restaurant distribution often correspond to developing economies or less urbanised regions, where access to international cuisines is limited.

Overall, the visualisation underscores how geography, economy, and tourism shape global dining availability, with major cities becoming central hubs for cross-cultural food experiences.

Distribution of Restaurant Ratings by Cuisine

The density chart enables interactive exploration of rating patterns across cuisines. Most restaurants globally achieve ratings between 4.0 and 4.8, demonstrating high customer satisfaction across cultural contexts. However, certain cuisines stand out for exceptional consistency and excellence.

Japanese and Italian cuisines often peak around 4.6, reflecting their consistent global appreciation and meticulous culinary standards. The chart also allows comparison across multiple cuisines simultaneously, offering insights into where diners experience the most satisfaction worldwide.

Top 10 Cuisines by Average Rating

The bar chart ranks the world’s most highly rated cuisines. Healthy cuisines dominate the leaderboard, followed by French and Pub styles — suggesting global appreciation for nutritious dining and casual yet high-quality dining environments.

The ranking suggests that cuisines rooted in traditional methods but open to innovation tend to achieve higher recognition, appealing to both local and international audiences.

Cuisine Popularity vs Quality

The scatterplot compares average restaurant ratings with the number of reviews, showing how popularity interacts with perceived quality. Heavily reviewed cuisines indicate widespread appeal, but high volume does not always guarantee top ratings.

Smaller cuisines, though less common, often achieve higher average ratings due to craftsmanship and niche appeal. This suggests that authenticity, care, and dining experience play an equally important role as scale in global culinary success.
